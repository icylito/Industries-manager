BRAINSTORM · ADMIN OVERVIEW

Turn the industries data into an admin Overview.

What the code allows, how it gets built, and where we are. Direction C won. The boards to the right are live prototypes on sample rows generated like your seed script, so click around. The handoff for the build is in OVERVIEW_HANDOFF.md.

1 · WHAT "ADMIN PAGE" IS TODAY
It is /account.
The screenshot is Your account: change password, sign out. The admin links are History, Fields, People, Requests. Nothing summarises the table, so there is no page to "redesign". We add one.
2 · THE DATA IS SMALL AND FLAT
Four columns, plus your custom ones.
Industry = name, sector, price, release, extra. Sector only has two options in config.toml, so a pie of sector alone is two slices. That drives most of the design.
3 · NO CHART LIBRARY, NO SHARED STORE
Angular and rxjs only.
Browse keeps its rows in a component signal and GET /industries returns every row, nothing is aggregated server side. A new page fetches the list itself or we lift the signal into IndustryApi.

Approach I'd take

1
New route /overview, admin guard. First link in the admin group of the nav. /account stays for password and sign out. No change to Browse.
2
Charts are hand-built SVG and CSS in the template. Pie is stroked circles, bars are flex divs. They read your CSS variables, so dark mode works untouched. A canvas library can't. Move to ECharts only if you later want zoom or brushing.
3
One selection signal drives everything. Click a slice or its legend row: bars show the selected share over the total, Top 3 and stats recompute. Click again to clear. Legend rows are real buttons for keyboard use.
4
Bridge to Browse. "Open 124 in Browse" links to /?sector=Public Sector. Browse reads no query params today, about 6 lines of TS, flagged before I touch it.
5
Colour is the real constraint. Black and white gives you lightness steps, enough for 4 slices. A fifth needs a pattern or one new token, which breaks "colours only from styles.css" and needs your yes.
